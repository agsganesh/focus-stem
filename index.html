About Focus Stem

Reach the potential you already have.

Most people aren't short on ability. They're short on focus, feedback and rest. Focus Stem is a study app made by students, for students, to help you do the work you're capable of, one block at a time.

Stemming is the steady work of turning roots into growth. A stem carries what grows underground up toward the light, so you can see it.

Sign in with your Gmail to save your progress and study with friends.

Why people don't reach their potential

Follow the root down.

ROOT 01

No clear starting point

Plans stay vague, so starting feels like one more decision you can't make.

ROOT 02

Constant distraction

Phones, tabs and notifications pull your attention away faster than you can get it back.

ROOT 03

Progress you can't see

Hours of real work leave no trace, so it feels like nothing is happening.

ROOT 04

Burnout and no rest

Pushing without breaks drains the energy that focus depends on.

ROOT 05

Going it alone

Without people working beside you, it's easy to stop and easy to drift.

How Focus Stem helps

Every root has an answer.

Root 01 · No clear starting point

Name it before you start

Stem asks what you're focusing on before each block, then keeps that label on your timer and your progress.

Root 02 · Constant distraction

One task, one timer

The timer keeps the time so you don't have to. Lo-fi music fills the room while you work.

Root 03 · Progress you can't see

Every minute counts

Your lifetime total grows with each block, and milestones from your first minute to one million mark how far you've come.

Root 04 · Burnout and no rest

Rest is built in

A short break follows each block and a long break follows every few, so you stop before you run out.

Root 05 · Going it alone

Study with friends

Send an invite link, see each other's totals on a leaderboard, and enter weekly challenges that end with a champion badge.
